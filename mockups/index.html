Metric strip with faces, inside the logo section

Hero (product loop or film) → logo section opens with a row of four small looping faces, each with its result number, then the logos → product sections. Bottom testimonials removed.

Faces and numbers in the first scroll without a new section: the logo block simply gets a top row. Numbers read before names, the way Maxio and Rillet lead with figures.

Single-story spotlight mid-page

Hero → logo strip → product sections → one large customer story at a time (video, number, quote, name), with arrows, replacing the text testimonials.

Gives each customer the full frame instead of a quarter of a row. Scales to many stories in a fixed height, and every story is one click from its full video.
